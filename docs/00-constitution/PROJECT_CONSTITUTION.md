# Project constitution

Status: ACCEPTED

World RPG is a fixed-world, long-form fantasy RPG total conversion built on Minecraft Java 1.21.1 and Fabric. Minecraft supplies the engine; World RPG supplies the game.

## Experience target

The first-character journey is intended to absorb hundreds of hours across many days or months. Leveling is the game, not a queue before the game.

The rhythm intentionally includes minutes of local travel, tens of minutes of regional travel, occasional first-time journeys near or beyond an hour, one-to-three-hour expeditions, quiet stretches, meaningful but non-constant combat, multi-session quest chains and gradual class mastery.

## Progression

- Current target: 100 levels.
- More frequent small levels are preferred to stretching a few levels into dead intervals.
- No global level scaling.
- Old content becomes easier gradually rather than instantly irrelevant.
- Gear scaling remains compressed enough for memorable items to last.
- Failure may cost position, time, consumables or repair, but should not casually erase hours of progress.

## World

- Main land is authored and persistent.
- Geography is gameplay.
- Roads, barriers, rivers, passes, settlements and transport matter.
- Quiet terrain is valid.
- Repeated travel improves because the player learns and unlocks infrastructure, not because unrestricted teleportation erases geography.
- Regions may contain content for widely separated level bands.

## Combat

- Target-based and ability-driven.
- Casts, channels, interrupts, effects, resources and positioning are central.
- Routine enemies may be relaxed.
- Enemy families need mechanical identity.
- Server authority is mandatory.
- Hundreds of abilities must compose from reusable primitives rather than bespoke classes.

## Content

Systems live in code. Game-specific content is data where practical. References use stable IDs. Generated bulk content is deterministic and inspectable. Unique content may break baseline formulas intentionally, visibly and testably.

## Scale rule

The project does not earn scale by creating files. It earns scale when infrastructure makes the next thousand files safe to create.
