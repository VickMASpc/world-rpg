# NPCs, mobs and AI

Status: DRAFT

NPC/mob definitions are separate from runtime entities and describe archetype, faction, stats, ability loadout, loot references, visuals and behavior configuration.

Baseline AI states to design: idle, patrol, alert/suspicious where useful, acquire, combat, evade/leash, return/reset, dead and respawn.

Enemy families need mechanical identity through casts, positioning, assist behavior, defenses, movement and ability packages rather than health/damage only.

Authored spawn areas will define actor pools, population constraints, respawn, patrol anchors, assist/leash geometry, world-state conditions and threat context.

Vanilla ambient spawning must not undermine designed encounter density in the RPG land.
