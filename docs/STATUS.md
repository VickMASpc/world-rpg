## Important interpretation

The current P4 encounter fixtures are **mechanical stress fixtures and provisional calibration evidence**.

They prove that the plant can express useful combat relationships.

They do not define final classes, enemies, dungeon roles, combat pacing or encounter design.

Likewise, P3 proof ability timings/ranges/costs are developer instrumentation, not production balance.

## Physical P3 harness readiness

The harness has been audited against the verification matrix rather than accepted on documentation alone.

The audit found and repaired proof-surface problems:

- timed Bolt was too short for reliable manual revalidation testing;
- target movement was needed to isolate range revalidation from source-movement interruption;
- proof-health setup was needed to make RPG exhaustion testable without changing game math;
- channel ticks needed visible resource-event evidence;
- LOS-blocked and beyond-crosshair server-condition cases needed an explicitly network-bypassing direct developer activation path.

The real network row still requires F7/F8 client input. Direct `room cast` feedback explicitly states that the network path was bypassed.

No manual P3 evidence is claimed yet.

## Why

World RPG's primary risk is not failure to build enough systems.

It is building an excellent system factory for a game whose lived experience drifts away from the original idea.

The conceptual design is now grounded enough to proceed, so the next uncertainty must be removed by **playing and measuring Minecraft**, not by writing another layer of architecture.

The plant remains subordinate to the game.
